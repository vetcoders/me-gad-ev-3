The three problems

parser-order — the shared parser reads every date month first

Every tenant on file writes the day first, so dates with a day of 12 or less come back flipped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July” (R-41200). A day of 13 or more fails or lands in the wrong month instead: October bookings shown as January (R-41615). This is the bug #4471 describes, and the queued fix addresses it — parse with the tenant’s locale, not month first.

hint-mismatch — booking-v3’s form hints MM/DD/YYYY, then reads the field day first

A different bug with the same symptom. Under its date field, booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY; people type what the hint says, and the form then parses their input the tenant’s way, day first. The queued parser fix does not touch this: the field needs one truth, hint and parse agreeing. It reaches only tenants on booking-v3, only through the form channel — which is why it hid comfortably inside the bigger group.

one-day-move — dates move by one day between viewers

Five reports the desk itself flagged in desk-notes.json describe no flip at all: the same meeting lands on Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto (R-41344), an invite dated before its own booking (R-41236), bookings one day off for one month only (R-41209). That is a date stored or rendered at a day boundary without its timezone — a different defect with a different fix, merged here because the subjects matched.

What we did about it

One ticket per problem, filed where the desk’s tracker can see them, each with the count of reports it covers from the table above:

  • vetcoders/me-gad-ev-3 · issues — parser-order, hint-mismatch, one-day-move, one issue each.

Lessons

  • A big ticket is usually one bug — but “merge on subject, read nothing” guarantees you will never notice when it is not. Sampling a handful of bodies per hundred merges would have caught all three.
  • Intake’s tag guesses from the reporter’s browser, not the tenant. It disagreed with the tenant-derived cause often enough that no triage should trust it alone.
  • Counts belong to components. The desk kept working while we wrote this, and the table will keep being true after the next export.
